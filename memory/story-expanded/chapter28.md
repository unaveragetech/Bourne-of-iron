# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 28: New Horizons

Post-cleanse, the ocean thrived. Coral reefs bloomed, fish multiplied, waters clear.

Moltly recovered in a healing spring, friends by his side.

Celebrations: feasts, dances, songs.

Human-ocean relations mended, treaties signed.

Moltly a legend, but humble.

New threats? None immediate, but vigilance.

Personal resolutions: Finley explored, Nemo adventured, Dory taught.

The Heart returned to rest, but Moltly its guardian.

Future bright, lessons learned.

They parted ways, but bonds eternal.

Moltly watched sunsets, content.
