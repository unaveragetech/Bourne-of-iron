# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 23: United Currents

To counter OceanDevour, alliances were key. The group journeyed to different seas: the Arctic, where polar bears and seals joined; the Pacific, with sharks and rays; the Atlantic, reuniting with old friends.

Moltly addressed councils, the Heart translating languages. "Together, we are strong."

Challenges: territorial disputes resolved through mediation.

They trained armies—fish formations, dolphin scouts.

Human allies expanded: scientists, politicians.

A summit underwater, using the Heart to create a bubble dome.

But spies infiltrated—OceanDevour agents posing as allies.

Betrayal struck: a trusted eel revealed as spy, stealing data.

Moltly pursued, recovering it with Finley's help.

The unity solidified, despite setbacks.

As they prepared, the Heart revealed visions of the future—clean oceans.

Moltly's resolve hardened.
