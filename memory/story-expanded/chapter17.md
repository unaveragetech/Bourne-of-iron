# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 17: River's Fury

The group ventured into the river, a murky waterway choked with algae and floating trash. Moltly rode on Finley's back, the Heart of the Ocean tucked safely in his claw. The current was strong, pulling them upstream towards the factories they had seen from afar.

Dory splashed excitedly, "This is like a big slide! But it smells funny."

Nemo clung to his father, wary of the unknown. Echo swam ahead, using her echolocation to navigate the treacherous path, dodging submerged branches and discarded fishing lines.

As they progressed, the pollution intensified. Dead fish floated belly-up, their eyes glazed over. "This is worse than the oil spill," Moltly muttered. The Heart glowed faintly, sensing the toxins.

They encountered river dwellers—a family of otters playing in the shallows, but even they looked sickly. "The water burns our eyes," one otter said. "We can't eat the fish anymore."

Moltly explained their quest, and the otters, led by a brave female named Ripple, joined them. "We'll guide you. The factories are just beyond the bend."

Deeper in, they faced a barrage of industrial waste pouring from pipes. Moltly used the Heart to create a barrier, purifying a small section. But the effort drained him.

Suddenly, a sewage overflow flooded the area, sweeping them towards a waterfall. They clung to rocks, hearts pounding.

Finley shouted, "Hold on!" As they tumbled over the edge, Moltly activated the Heart, slowing their fall with a cushion of water.

At the bottom, they met a council of turtles, ancient guardians of the river. "You seek to cleanse?" the eldest turtle, Sage, asked.

"Yes," Moltly replied. "With your help."

The turtles shared wisdom of the land's corruption, teaching Moltly to harness the Heart's energy more efficiently.

But threats emerged—hunters with boats, poaching turtles. The group ambushed them, using illusions and distractions.

As dawn broke, they neared the factories, the air thick with smoke.
