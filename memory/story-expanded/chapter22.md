# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 22: Depths of Deceit

OceanDevour's operations were vast, with rigs drilling into the ocean floor, extracting minerals at the cost of ecosystems. The group approached stealthily, Echo's sonar mapping the structures.

Moltly felt the vibrations—explosions shattering coral. "We must destroy their rigs," he declared.

They infiltrated: Finley swam into pipes, Nemo distracted with acrobatics, Dory confused workers with visions, Echo disrupted communications.

Moltly confronted the CEO via hologram. "Your greed destroys us all."

The CEO laughed, activating defenses—nets and spears.

A battle ensued. Moltly used the Heart to collapse a rig, but the effort drained him.

They rescued trapped creatures, gaining more allies.

Inside, they found experiments—mutated fish. "This is monstrous," Lena said.

They sabotaged the main computer, exposing data.

Escape was narrow—pursued by submarines.

Back at base, they analyzed the data, planning a global strike.

The Heart pulsed warningly, nearing overload.
