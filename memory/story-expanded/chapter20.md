# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 20: Breach and Broadcast

Under the cover of night, the infiltration began. Alex smuggled the group into the factory via a service entrance, using his ID badge. Moltly clung to his backpack, the Heart hidden inside.

Inside, the facility hummed with activity. Conveyor belts moved barrels of chemicals, and workers monitored screens. "Stay hidden," Alex whispered.

They split: Finley and Nemo distracted guards by knocking over boxes, Dory created illusions of shadows, Echo jammed signals with her sonar.

Moltly, with Lena, headed to the control room. Lena hacked into the system, uploading evidence to the internet.

But Greedwell appeared, alerted by an alarm. "What's this nonsense?" he roared.

Moltly emerged, Heart glowing. "Your pollution ends now!"

A chase ensued. Greedwell's men fired nets, but Moltly dodged, using the Heart to shield.

They reached the roof, where a broadcast antenna stood. Lena connected her device, streaming live: "This is the truth about Greedwell Industries!"

Footage of polluted rivers, dead animals, and the Heart's purification played.

The world watched. Protests erupted outside.

Greedwell lunged, but Moltly pinned him with a claw illusion.

Police arrived, arresting Greedwell.

The factory shut down temporarily.

The group escaped, heroes.

But the Heart dimmed further—global cleanse neared.
