# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 18: Factory Shadows

The factories loomed like giants, their smokestacks belching gray clouds into the sky. The group approached cautiously, hiding in the reeds along the riverbank. Moltly's claws trembled with anticipation and fear. "This is it," he said. "The heart of the pollution."

Echo scanned ahead. "I hear machinery and voices. Many humans."

They decided to split: Finley, Nemo, and Dory would scout the perimeter, while Moltly, with the Heart, attempted to infiltrate through a drainage pipe.

Inside the pipe, it was dark and claustrophobic, sludge coating the walls. Moltly slipped through, the Heart's light guiding him. He emerged into a massive tank, where chemicals mixed with water, creating toxic brew.

A worker spotted him—a young man in overalls, dropping his clipboard in shock. "A lobster? In here?"

Moltly, using the Heart's power, projected an illusion of himself speaking. "Stop the pollution! You're killing the ocean!"

The worker, confused, called for help. Guards arrived, but Moltly hid in the shadows.

Meanwhile, outside, Finley and the others distracted by causing ripples in the water, attracting attention.

Inside, Moltly sabotaged pipes, releasing harmless steam. But he was cornered. Using the Heart, he created a fog, blinding the guards.

He escaped back to the pipe, reuniting with friends. But the worker followed, intrigued. "Who are you?"

Moltly revealed himself, and the worker, named Alex, listened. "I work here, but I hate it. The boss cares only about money."

Alex agreed to help, sneaking out information about the company's plans.

As they fled, alarms blared. They raced down the river, pursued by boats.

The Heart's power flickered, needing rest.

But they gained a human ally, a glimmer of hope.
