# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 19: Allies in Disguise

With Alex as their guide, the group retreated to a hidden cove, away from the factory's reach. The human sat awkwardly on the shore, his boots sinking into the sand. "I can't believe I'm talking to sea creatures," he said, shaking his head.

Moltly placed the Heart on a rock, its glow illuminating the night. "You believe now, don't you? The ocean is dying because of places like yours."

Alex nodded. "My boss, Mr. Greedwell, dumps waste illegally. I have documents—emails, reports. But I need proof from the sea."

The group discussed strategy. "We need to rally humans too," Finley suggested. "Like the environmentalists I've heard about."

Echo chimed in, "I can communicate with whales who know of activists."

They sent Echo to summon help. Meanwhile, Moltly and Alex reviewed the documents. The company planned to expand, drilling into the seabed.

Nemo and Dory played lookout, spotting patrols.

As dawn approached, a boat arrived—activists led by a woman named Lena. "Alex called us. Show us what you've got."

Moltly demonstrated the Heart, purifying a polluted pool. Lena gasped. "This is incredible. We'll expose Greedwell."

They formed a plan: infiltrate the factory again, plant evidence, and broadcast live.

But risks loomed—Greedwell's security was tight.

Moltly trained with the Heart, learning new abilities, like controlling tides.

The alliance strengthened, blending sea and land.

As they prepared, a storm brewed, mirroring their turmoil.
