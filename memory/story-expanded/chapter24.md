# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 24: Tide of Battle

The assault on OceanDevour commenced at dawn. Fleets of sea creatures charged the rigs, whales ramming hulls, schools of fish overwhelming nets.

Moltly led the charge, Heart blazing. "For the ocean!"

Defenses fired harpoons, but illusions deflected.

Inside rigs, sabotage teams planted explosives—natural algae blooms that corroded metal.

Lena coordinated from surface, jamming signals.

Climactic duel: Moltly vs. CEO in mech suit.

Heart vs. technology—the mech faltered, CEO ejected.

Rigs collapsed, but environmental damage lingered.

Victory, but costly—losses mourned.

The group retreated, Heart dimming.

Global attention shifted, laws changed.

But the cleanse remained.
