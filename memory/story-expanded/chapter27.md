# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 27: Purification Unleashed

The cleanse started at the deepest trench. Moltly raised the Heart, chanting ancient words learned from visions.

Energy erupted, waves of light spreading, dissolving toxins, repairing damage.

Oceans glowed, pollutants vaporized.

Creatures felt renewal, swimming freely.

But backlash: storms raged, earthquakes shook.

Moltly held steady, friends anchoring him.

Humans on surface monitored, aiding with technology.

Phases: water cleanse, then air, then land.

Global effect—rivers cleared, skies blue.

Enemies' bases crumbled.

Moltly weakened, but persevered.

Climax: confronting a dark entity, embodiment of pollution.

Heart banished it.

Triumph, oceans pristine.

Moltly collapsed, exhausted.
