# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 30: Guardian's Call

Peace shattered by a new menace—alien invaders polluting from space, their ships crashing, leaking exotic toxins.

Moltly activated the Heart, rallying allies.

Battle in the stars? No, underwater bases.

Confronted invaders, using Heart to communicate.

Alliance formed, aliens repentant.

Cleaned cosmic waste.

Moltly's heroism expanded.

Returned to Earth, stronger bonds.

The story evolved, Moltly eternal guardian.
