## Chapter 26: Gathering Storms

Preparation for the cleanse intensified. The group traveled to power spots: volcanoes, trenches, where energies converged.

The Heart grew brighter, absorbing essences.

Friends supported, sharing burdens.

As the moment approached, tension peaked.

The world watched, hopeful.

Moltly centered himself, ready to unleash.
