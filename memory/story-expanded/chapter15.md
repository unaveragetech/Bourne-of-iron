# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 15: Surface Confrontation

Breaking through the oil spill, the group ascended to the surface layers, where sunlight pierced the waves and human ships dotted the horizon. These vessels, massive and indifferent, dumped toxic waste and trawled nets that ensnared everything.

The Heart of the Ocean's light attracted attention—a research vessel spotted the glow, sending divers. Moltly hid the Heart, but the divers' bubbles disturbed the water.

"We must protect the Heart and expose the pollution," Moltly said. They sabotaged nets, freeing trapped fish, and used the Heart to neutralize waste plumes.

A diver grabbed Moltly, but he pinched the suit, causing retreat. Finley and Nemo distracted with bites, Dory confused divers with illusions, Echo echoed warnings, allies assisted.

The ship retreated, but more came. They lured them to a whirlpool created by the Heart, sinking minor threats.

As they neared the shore, the true storm brewed—factories spewing smoke, rivers carrying poison. The Heart's final use approached, to cleanse globally.

But a traitor among humans? No, focus on sea. They rallied sea creatures for a united front.
