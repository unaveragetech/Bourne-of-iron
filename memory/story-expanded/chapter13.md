# The Adventures of Moltly the Lobster

## Chapter 13: Polluted Depths

Emerging from the abyss, the group entered the mid-depth waters, a realm scarred by human pollution. The water was murky, choked with floating debris—plastic bags ensnaring fish, oil slicks glistening like poison, and chemical plumes burning gills. Schools of deformed fish swam erratically, their colors faded.

The Heart of the Ocean pulsed urgently, its light dimmed by the toxins. "We must cleanse as we go," Moltly declared.

They encountered a pod of sick dolphins, coughing and weak. Echo recognized kin, leading healing. The Heart's energy purified a small area, reviving life.

But predators adapted to pollution—a mutated shark, armored in trash, attacked. Its hide impervious to bites, it rammed the group.

Teamwork prevailed: Moltly pinned it with strength, Finley navigated gaps, Dory lured it into a current trap, Nemo poked eyes, new allies assisted. Elder Shell chanted protection, Swift evaded, Guardian shielded.

Defeating the shark, they continued, spreading the Heart's influence. Fish thanked them, joining the cause.

The surface neared, but a massive oil spill blocked the way. They devised a plan to breach it, the Heart's power their key to salvation.
